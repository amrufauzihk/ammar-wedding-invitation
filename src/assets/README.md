# Wedding Invitation Assets

Replace these placeholder files with your own content before deploying.

## Music

Background music is streamed from YouTube. Update the video ID in `src/lib/constants.ts`:

- `BACKGROUND_MUSIC_YOUTUBE_ID` — Current: [YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2e0BMACvymo)

## Images

- `public/images/groom-placeholder.svg` — Groom photo
- `public/images/bride-placeholder.svg` — Bride photo
- `public/images/gallery-1.svg` through `gallery-6.svg` — Gallery photos

Recommended: use `.jpg` or `.webp` for photos and update paths in `src/lib/constants.ts`.

## Content

Edit wedding details, dates, locations, bank info, and love story in `src/lib/constants.ts`.
